# s25-family-detection
Almost every single family has certain traits that you consider a core part of being a member of said family by blood. Be it an aspect in regards to their personality, mental stability, 
or a physical aspect. For my family, at least on my father's side, we have something called the Kirkpatrick nose. A big ol fat schnoz in the center of the head. Luckily this skipped me, but a conversation about it 
this past Christmas got me thinking about what other facial aspects can be found in common with members of my father's and mother's family. 
That's the goal of this project, to try and calculate chance of kinship via facial analysis utilizing machine learning techniques.

In particular this project uses siamese neutral networks to compare faces and determine if they belong to the same family. The data utilized for training is from the Northeastern University's project Families in the Wild.
The model is trained on a dataset derived from the project's compiled photos. Photos for testing the model were personally compiled by me and involve random stock photos from the internet and also personal photos of my own family members.
